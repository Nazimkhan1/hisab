Hisab Kitab kaise use karein: poori guide

Hisab Kitab Guide · NextGrade

Hisab Kitab ek free app hai jisme aap roz ka kharcha, aamdani aur udhaar (len-den) ek jagah likh sakte hain. Is guide mein shuru se aakhir tak sab kuch step by step hai.

1. App kholiye aur install kariye

Phone ke Chrome mein hisab.nextgrade.in kholiye. Neeche “Hisab Kitab app install kariye” aaye to Install karo dabaiye. iPhone par Safari mein Share button dabakar “Add to Home Screen” chuniye. Ab app home screen se seedha khulega, bina internet ke bhi.

2. Pehli entry jodiye

  1. Upar entry ka type chuniye: Kharcha, Aamdani, Udhaar diya, Udhaar liya, Wapas mila ya Wapas diya.
  2. Rakam likhiye, jaise 250.
  3. Kharcha ya aamdani ho to category chuniye (Khana-peena, Rent, Bills, Travel…). Udhaar ho to us vyakti ka naam likhiye.
  4. Tareekh apne aap aaj ki hoti hai; chahein to badal dijiye.
  5. Note optional hai, jaise “sabzi” ya “bijli bill”.
  6. Entry jodo dabaiye. Neeche hara message aayega: entry save ho gayi.

3. Bol kar likhiye

Form ke upar 🎤 Bol kar likho dabaiye aur boliye, jaise “Sabzi 250” ya “Ramesh ko 2000 udhaar diya”. Form apne aap bhar jaata hai. Ek saath kai cheezein bhi bol sakte hain: “Pyaj 1 kg 60, aalu 1 kg 30”. Iske baare mein poori guide yahan hai.

4. Roz ka hisab dekhiye

Roz ka hisab tab mein us mahine ki saari entries tareekh ke hisab se dikhti hain. Upar “Kharcha kahan gaya” mein category ke hisab se pata chalta hai ki paisa sabse zyada kahan gaya.

5. Len-den (udhaar)

Len-den tab mein har vyakti ka baaki hisab dikhta hai: kisse kitna lena hai aur kisko kitna dena hai. Naam par tap karke “Wapas mila likho” se paisa lautne ki entry jaldi likh sakte hain.

6. Statement

Statement tab bilkul bank passbook jaisa hai. Mahina, saal ya koi bhi date range chuniye; shuru ka balance, aaya, gaya aur har entry ke baad ka balance dikhega. Excel (CSV) download ya PDF/print bhi kar sakte hain.

7. Google se login (data safe rakhiye)

Upar Google se login dabaiye. Aapka hisab aapke apne Google Drive ke “Hisab Kitab” folder mein save hota hai, aur phone badalne par bhi wapas mil jaata hai. App banane wala aapka data nahi dekh sakta. Detail: aapka data kaise safe hai.

Aur padhiye

  • Ghar ka monthly budget kaise banayein (aasaan tarika)
  • Udhaar ka hisab kaise rakhein taaki rishta bhi bana rahe
  • Roz ke kharche mein paise bachane ke 12 aasaan tarike
  • 50-30-20 budget rule kya hai? Hindi mein samjhiye
