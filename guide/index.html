Hisab Kitab Guide

Ghar ka budget, udhaar ka hisab, paise bachane ke tarike aur Hisab Kitab app ki poori jaankari, aasaan bhasha mein.

Hisab Kitab kaise use karein: poori guide

Hisab Kitab app mein kharcha, aamdani aur udhaar kaise likhein, statement kaise dekhein aur Google Drive mein kaise save karein.

Ghar ka monthly budget kaise banayein (aasaan tarika)

Ghar ka mahine ka budget banane ka aasaan tarika: aamdani likhiye, zaroori kharche alag kariye, bachat pehle rakhiye aur har hafte check kariye.

Udhaar ka hisab kaise rakhein taaki rishta bhi bana rahe

Dost, rishtedar ya dukaandar ke saath udhaar ka saaf hisab kaise rakhein: tareekh, rakam, wapsi aur baaki, sab ek jagah.

Roz ke kharche mein paise bachane ke 12 aasaan tarike

Ghar ke roz ke kharche mein paise bachane ke 12 aasaan aur practical tarike: bijli, khana, mobile, shopping aur aadat.

50-30-20 budget rule kya hai? Hindi mein samjhiye

50-30-20 budget rule aasaan bhasha mein: aamdani ka 50% zaroorat, 30% chahat aur 20% bachat. Misaal aur Indian ghar ke liye tips.

Apna hisab bank statement jaisa kaise dekhein (PDF aur Excel)

Hisab Kitab ke Statement tab se mahine, saal ya kisi bhi date range ka bank passbook jaisa hisab dekhiye aur PDF ya Excel mein download kariye.

Bol kar hisab likhiye: voice entry ki poori guide

Hisab Kitab mein mic dabakar Hinglish, Hindi, Bangla ya Urdu mein bol kar kharcha aur udhaar likhiye, ek saath kai cheezein bhi.

Hisab Kitab mein aapka data kaise safe hai

Hisab Kitab mein aapka hisab sirf aapke phone ya aapke apne Google Drive mein rehta hai. App banane wala bhi nahi dekh sakta. Poori jaankari.
