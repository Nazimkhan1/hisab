Roz ke kharche mein paise bachane ke 12 aasaan tarike

Hisab Kitab Guide · NextGrade

Bachat ke liye badi kurbani nahi, chhoti aadatein kaafi hain. Ye tarike kisi bhi ghar mein aaj se shuru ho sakte hain.

Khana aur ration

  1. List banakar bazaar jaiye. Bina list ke bazaar jaane par zyada kharcha hota hai.
  2. Mahine ka ration ek saath lijiye. Aata, chawal, daal, tel thok mein sasta padta hai.
  3. Mausam ki sabzi aur fal lijiye. Wo saste bhi hote hain aur taaze bhi.
  4. Bahar khana hafte mein tay kariye. Jaise sirf weekend par.

Bills

  1. Bijli: khaali kamre ki light-pankha band, purane bulb ki jagah LED.
  2. Mobile plan check kariye. Kai log zyada data ka plan lete hain jo use hi nahi hota.
  3. Subscription dekhiye. Jo app ya OTT kaam ka nahi, use band kariye.

Shopping

  1. 24 ghante ka niyam: koi cheez pasand aaye to ek din ruk jaiye. Zaroorat hogi to kal bhi hogi.
  2. Sale ka matlab bachat nahi. Jo cheez chahiye hi nahi, wo sasti ho kar bhi mehngi hai.

Aadatein

  1. Har kharcha likhiye. Likhne se hi pata chalta hai ki chhote kharche milkar kitne bade ho jaate hain. Hisab Kitab mein “Kharcha kahan gaya” turant dikha deta hai.
  2. Salary aate hi bachat alag kariye. Pehle bachat, phir kharcha.
  3. Har mahine ka statement dekhiye. Pichle mahine se tulna kariye aur ek category mein thoda kam karne ka lakshya rakhiye.
