Ghar ka monthly budget kaise banayein (aasaan tarika)

Hisab Kitab Guide · NextGrade

Mahine ke aakhir mein paise kahan chale gaye, ye sawal har ghar mein aata hai. Budget ka matlab kanjoosi nahi, balki ye pehle se tay karna hai ki paisa kahan jayega. Ye 6 step ka tarika kisi bhi aamdani par chalta hai.

Step 1: Mahine ki asli aamdani likhiye

Salary, dukaan ki kamai, kiraya, jo bhi paisa haath mein aata hai, sab jodiye. Tax ya EMI katne ke baad jo haath mein aata hai wahi ginni chahiye.

Step 2: Zaroori kharche alag likhiye

Ye wo kharche hain jo har mahine aate hi hain: rent, bijli-paani, gas, ration, school fees, EMI, dawai, mobile recharge. Inhe pehle likh lijiye, kyunki ye kam-zyada nahi hote.

Step 3: Bachat ko kharcha maaniye

Sabse badi galti ye hai ki jo bach gaya wahi bachat. Ulta kariye: salary aate hi ek rakam (jaise 10%) alag rakh dijiye, jaise koi bill ho. Thodi rakam bhi har mahine judkar badi ban jaati hai.

Step 4: Baaki paisa “chahat” ke liye

Bahar khana, shopping, ghoomna, ye sab bache hue paise se kariye. Isse zaroori kharchon ke liye kabhi kami nahi padegi.

Step 5: Har kharcha likhiye

Budget tabhi kaam karta hai jab pata ho ki asal mein kitna kharch hua. Chhota sa kharcha bhi likhiye: chai, auto, recharge. Hisab Kitab mein ek entry 2 second mein ho jaati hai, ya bol kar bhi likh sakte hain.

Step 6: Har hafte 5 minute check kariye

Hafte mein ek baar “Kharcha kahan gaya” dekhiye. Kisi category mein zyada ho raha ho to agle hafte wahan thoda kam kariye. Mahine ke aakhir mein Statement dekhkar agle mahine ka budget aur behtar banaiye.

Ek misaal

Maan lijiye aamdani ₹30,000 hai: zaroori kharche ₹18,000, bachat ₹3,000 (10%), aur baaki ₹9,000 khaane-peene, ghoomne aur dusri chahaton ke liye. Isi tarah ka ek seedha niyam hai 50-30-20 rule.
