50-30-20 budget rule kya hai? Hindi mein samjhiye

Hisab Kitab Guide · NextGrade

50-30-20 ek seedha budget niyam hai jo aamdani ko teen hisson mein baant deta hai. Ise yaad rakhna aasaan hai, isliye ye shuru karne ke liye achha hai.

Niyam kya kehta hai

  • 50% zaroorat (Needs): rent, ration, bijli-paani, school fees, EMI, dawai, aana-jaana.
  • 30% chahat (Wants): bahar khana, shopping, ghoomna, OTT, gadgets.
  • 20% bachat (Savings): emergency fund, RD/FD, SIP, ya qarz jaldi chukana.

Misaal

Aamdani ₹40,000 hai to: zaroorat ₹20,000, chahat ₹12,000, bachat ₹8,000.

Agar 50% mein zaroorat poori na ho?

Bade shehron mein sirf rent hi aamdani ka bada hissa le leta hai. Aisa ho to niyam ko apne hisab se badliye, jaise 60-25-15 ya 70-20-10. Asli baat ye hai ki bachat ka hissa pehle tay ho aur har mahine ho, chahe chhota ho.

Hisab Kitab ke saath kaise lagayein

  1. Har kharcha category ke saath likhiye.
  2. Mahine ke aakhir mein Roz ka hisab mein “Kharcha kahan gaya” dekhiye.
  3. Rent, Bills, Ghar ka saman, Dawai, Padhai jodkar “zaroorat” nikaliye; Shopping, bahar khana jaisi cheezein “chahat” mein.
  4. Statement mein mahine ka aaya aur gaya dekhkar bachat ka pata chalta hai.

Budget banane ka poora tarika: ghar ka monthly budget.

Aur padhiye

  • Hisab Kitab kaise use karein: poori guide
  • Ghar ka monthly budget kaise banayein (aasaan tarika)
  • Udhaar ka hisab kaise rakhein taaki rishta bhi bana rahe
  • Roz ke kharche mein paise bachane ke 12 aasaan tarike
