Apna hisab bank statement jaisa kaise dekhein (PDF aur Excel)

Hisab Kitab Guide · NextGrade

Bank statement mein har entry ke baad balance dikhta hai, jisse hisab samajhna aasaan hota hai. Hisab Kitab ka Statement tab aapke ghar ke hisab ko bilkul aise hi dikhata hai.

Statement kaise kholein

App mein neeche Statement tab dabaiye (computer par upar tabs mein).

Samay chuniye

  • Mahina: jaise November 2026.
  • Saal: poore saal ka hisab.
  • Date range: kisi bhi tareekh se kisi bhi tareekh tak.

“Kaun si entries” se sab entries, sirf roz ka hisab, ya sirf len-den chun sakte hain.

Kya-kya dikhta hai

  • Shuru ka balance: chune hue samay se pehle ka kul hisab.
  • Aaya (Jama) aur Gaya (Nikasi) ka total.
  • Aakhri balance.
  • Har entry: tareekh, vivran, gaya, aaya aur us entry ke baad ka balance.

Download aur print

Excel (CSV) download se file Excel ya Google Sheets mein khulti hai. PDF / Print se sirf statement print hota hai; phone par “Save as PDF” chuniye. Ye file kisi ko bhejni ho, ya apne record ke liye rakhni ho, dono ke kaam aati hai.

Aur padhiye

  • Hisab Kitab kaise use karein: poori guide
  • Ghar ka monthly budget kaise banayein (aasaan tarika)
  • Udhaar ka hisab kaise rakhein taaki rishta bhi bana rahe
  • Roz ke kharche mein paise bachane ke 12 aasaan tarike
