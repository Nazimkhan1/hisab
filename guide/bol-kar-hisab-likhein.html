Bol kar hisab likhiye: voice entry ki poori guide

Hisab Kitab Guide · NextGrade

Type karna mushkil lage to bol dijiye. Hisab Kitab aapki baat sunkar form khud bhar deta hai. Aap check karke “Entry jodo” dabaate hain, isliye bina dekhe kuch save nahi hota.

Kaise use karein

  1. Form ke upar 🎤 Bol kar likho dabaiye.
  2. Pehli baar phone mic ki permission maangega; Allow kariye.
  3. Boliye. Rukte hi app samajh kar form bhar dega.
  4. Check kariye aur Entry jodo dabaiye.

Kya bol sakte hain

  • “Sabzi 250 rupaye”: kharcha, Khana-peena
  • “Kal petrol 300”: kal ki tareekh, Travel
  • “Salary 35000 aayi”: aamdani
  • “Ramesh ko 2000 udhaar diya”: udhaar diya, Ramesh
  • “Salman se 1000 wapas mila”: wapas mila
  • “Do hazar kiraya”: ₹2,000, Rent

Ek saath kai cheezein

Bazaar se aakar ek hi baar mein boliye: “Pyaj 1 kg 60, aalu ek kg 30, bhindi ek kg 70”. Ek list khulti hai jisme har cheez ka naam, matra, rakam aur category hoti hai. Galat ho to badal dijiye ya tick hata dijiye, phir “3 entry jodo” dabaiye.

Kuch tips

  • Saaf aur thoda dheere boliye; shor kam ho to behtar.
  • Bolkar likhne ke liye internet chahiye.
  • Awaaz text banane ke liye browser ki speech service (Chrome mein Google) use hoti hai. Ye sirf mic chalu hone par hota hai, aur awaaz hum tak nahi aati.
