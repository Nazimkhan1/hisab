Udhaar ka hisab kaise rakhein taaki rishta bhi bana rahe

Hisab Kitab Guide · NextGrade

Udhaar dena-lena aam baat hai, par yaad na rehne par jhagda bhi isi se hota hai. “Maine to lauta diye the” ya “kitne baaki the?” jaise sawal tab nahi aate jab hisab saaf likha ho.

Har udhaar turant likhiye

Jis din paisa diya ya liya, usi din likhiye: kisko, kitna, kab aur kyun. Baad mein yaad karne par galti hoti hai.

Wapsi bhi likhiye

Paisa thoda-thoda lautta hai, isliye har kisht alag likhiye. Isse baaki rakam hamesha sahi rehti hai.

Hisab Kitab mein kaise likhein

  • Udhaar diya: aapne kisi ko paise diye, wo lautayega.
  • Udhaar liya: aapne kisi se paise liye, aapko lautane hain.
  • Wapas mila: jisko diya tha, usne lautaya.
  • Wapas diya: jisse liya tha, usko lautaya.

Len-den tab mein har vyakti ke saamne “Lena hai ₹…” ya “Dena hai ₹…” dikhta hai. Hisab barabar hone par “Hisab barabar” likha aata hai.

Bol kar bhi likh sakte hain

🎤 dabakar boliye: “Ramesh ko 2000 udhaar diya” ya “Salman se 1000 wapas mila”. Naam, rakam aur type apne aap bhar jaate hain.

Rishte ke liye kuch baatein

  • Udhaar dete waqt hi wapsi ka andaaza tay kar lijiye.
  • Utna hi dijiye jitna na lautne par bhi aapka kaam na ruke.
  • Yaad dilana ho to pyaar se, likhe hue hisab ke saath. Saaf hisab rishte bachata hai.
