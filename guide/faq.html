Aksar pooche jaane wale sawal

Kya Hisab Kitab free hai?

Haan, poori tarah free hai. Koi feature paid nahi hai.

Kya internet ke bina chalega?

Haan. Install karne ke baad app bina internet ke khulta hai aur entries phone mein save hoti hain. Internet aane par Google Drive mein sync ho jaati hain (agar login kiya hai). Bol kar likhne ke liye internet chahiye.

Phone badal liya to purana hisab milega?

Agar aapne Google se login kiya tha to haan: naye phone par usi Gmail se login kariye, hisab Drive se wapas aa jayega. Bina login ke data sirf purane phone mein hota hai; uske liye ⋮ menu se “Backup lo” kariye.

Kya app banane wala mera hisab dekh sakta hai?

Nahi. App ka koi server ya database nahi hai. Data sirf aapke phone ya aapke Google Drive mein rehta hai. Detail yahan.

Galat entry kaise hatayein?

Entry ke saamne × dabaiye, phir “Haan, hatao”. Login ho to Drive se bhi hat jaati hai.

App install kaise karein?

Android par Chrome mein link kholiye aur “Install karo” dabaiye, ya ⋮ menu se “Install app”. iPhone par Safari mein Share dabakar “Add to Home Screen”.

Kaun si bhashayein hain?

Hinglish, English, हिन्दी, বাংলা aur اردو. ⋮ menu mein “Bhasha” se badaliye.

Statement PDF mein kaise milega?

Statement tab mein samay chuniye aur “PDF / Print” dabaiye, phir “Save as PDF” chuniye. Excel ke liye “Excel (CSV) download”.

Mic kaam nahi kar raha

Browser ko mic ki permission dijiye (site settings mein Microphone: Allow). Internet chalu rakhiye. Firefox mein voice entry nahi chalti; Chrome use kariye.

Data kaise delete karein?

Google Drive mein “Hisab Kitab” folder delete kariye, aur chahein to myaccount.google.com/permissions se app ki access hata dijiye.
