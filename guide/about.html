About

Hisab Kitab ek free app hai jo NextGrade ne banaya hai, ek chhota sa independent project jise India ke software engineer Nazim Khan chalate hain. Maqsad simple hai: roz ke kaam ki chhoti apps banana jo har phone par chalein, apni bhasha mein hon, aur use karne walon ki privacy ki izzat karein.

Hisab Kitab kyun

Bahut se ghar aaj bhi kharcha aur udhaar diary mein likhte hain. Hisab Kitab wahi diary phone mein laata hai: 2 second mein entry, bol kar likhna, mahine ka hisab, udhaar ka baaki aur bank jaisa statement.

Hamare usool

  • Aapka data aapka: koi server nahi; hisab aapke phone ya aapke Google Drive mein.
  • Sabke liye free: har feature free hai. Project chalane ke liye kuch pages par Google ke ads dikh sakte hain.
  • Aapki bhasha: Hinglish, English, हिन्दी, বাংলা aur اردو.
